### 01 &nbsp; Optical sensing with dual-comb spectroscopy

**University of Electro-Communications, Japan · Research Exchange · Summer 2026**

Built and integrated a hollow-core fiber setup into an existing dual-comb spectroscopy system. Refined fiber cleaving, fusion splicing, and butt-coupling to achieve **65% transmission efficiency** at an acceptable signal-to-noise ratio. Used phase-spectrum measurements to characterize group refractive index and dispersion, including their response to changing gas pressure.

`Hollow-core fiber` `Dual-comb spectroscopy` `Optical coupling` `MATLAB`

### 02 &nbsp; High-power laser delivery through hollow-core fibers

**The Photonics Institute, NTU · Undergraduate Research · 2025–2026**

Developed measurement setups to investigate how fiber interface angles affect back-reflection and transmission. Achieved **−35.28 dB back-reflection** with **0.63 dB insertion loss** at a 1.4° interface angle, balancing reflection suppression against transmission performance.

`High-power lasers` `Fiber processing` `Fusion splicing` `Optical characterization`

### 03 &nbsp; Analog integrated circuit design

**Georgia Institute of Technology · ECE 4430 · Fall 2026 · In progress**

Extracted EKV model parameters from measured GF180 CMOS transistor characteristics using MATLAB. Designed, simulated, and laid out analog building blocks, including source followers, common-source amplifiers, differential pairs, and a nine-transistor transconductance amplifier.

`GF180 CMOS` `Xschem` `Magic` `SPICE` `DRC / LVS` `Post-layout simulation`

### 04 &nbsp; Optical mode tracking

**Temasek Laboratories, NTU · Research Internship · 2026**

Explored Python-based image processing and numerical simulation for identifying and tracking optical modes in hollow-core fibers across different wavelengths.

`Python` `Image processing` `Numerical simulation` `Photonics`
